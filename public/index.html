Classification Policy & Assumptions

  • Theme: Assigned single dominant theme from Performance, Usability, Authentication, Reporting, Search, Pricing, Onboarding, Other.
  • Sentiment: Positive (praise/satisfaction), Negative (dissatisfaction/issues), Neutral (requests/observations without strong emotion).
  • Urgency: High (blocked core workflow / rollout blocker), Medium (meaningful friction without total blockage), Low (praise / non-blocking suggestion).
  • Deterministic Metrics: All dashboard counts, percentages, and theme totals are calculated in JavaScript from item classifications.
